# Trust / credentials section

This is the single highest fabrication-risk section on the site
(`CLAUDE.md` §11 "Trust", §28). Every fact here must be either verified
by the user or a bracketed placeholder — no exceptions, no "reasonable
sounding" numbers.

## Shape

1. **Professional status** — licensing/registration facts, stated
   plainly, no superlatives needed (the fact itself carries the weight).
2. **Methodology** — one short paragraph on how the firm works
   (legality, proportionality, confidentiality) rather than years/case
   counts if those aren't confirmed yet.
3. **Confidentiality commitment** — a calm, concrete sentence, not a
   generic "we value your privacy" line.

## Required placeholders until real data exists

```
[NOMBRE DEL DETECTIVE]        [NOMBRE DEL DESPACHO]
[TIP]                         [RNSP]
[AÑOS DE EXPERIENCIA]         [CIUDAD]
[COLEGIACIÓN / MEMBRESÍA]     [CERTIFICACIONES]
```

## Do

- Write the methodology paragraph even when the numeric facts are
  placeholders — process/legality claims don't need to wait on real data
  the way credentials do.
- Use "detective privado con licencia" / "número de TIP" as the accurate
  Spanish regulatory terms — don't invent alternative terminology.

## Don't

- Don't write "más de X años" with any number, even a low or
  conservative-sounding one, unless the user confirmed it.
- Don't add a testimonials/reviews carousel with placeholder quotes —
  omit the section entirely until real testimonials exist, or state
  explicitly in the draft that it's pending.
- Don't claim membership in an association, a certification, or an
  award without the user having named it.

## Example rewrite

> Fabricated: "Con más de 15 años de experiencia y cientos de casos
> resueltos con éxito, somos el despacho de referencia en Madrid."

> Correct (pending real data): "[NOMBRE DEL DETECTIVE], detective
> privado con licencia (TIP [TIP], RNSP [RNSP]), con
> [AÑOS DE EXPERIENCIA] de experiencia en investigación privada en
> [CIUDAD]."
