# Hero headline

## Shape

1. **Statement of value** — one or two short lines naming the gap
   between suspicion and evidence (`CLAUDE.md` §11 "Problem/Insight").
   Concrete verb, no adjective stacking.
2. **Supporting line** — one sentence explaining what the firm does,
   in plain terms a first-time visitor understands immediately.
3. **Primary CTA** — "Cuéntanos tu caso" or a calm variant.
4. **Secondary CTA** (optional) — "Consulta confidencial".

## Do

- Name the outcome the visitor wants (information, clarity, a decision)
  — not the drama of getting there.
- Keep the headline scannable in under 3 seconds.
- Let the supporting line do the explaining; the headline states, it
  doesn't narrate.

## Don't

- Don't open with a question designed to create anxiety
  ("¿Sospechas que te engañan?") — informative and calm beats
  sensationalist, per `CLAUDE.md` §6.
- Don't use "misión", "operación", "descubre la verdad ahora" or any
  other thriller-register phrase — the linter will flag these.
- Don't promise a result ("resultados garantizados") — that's
  `legal-guardrails` territory and it will be flagged there too.
