# CTA / conversion copy

`CLAUDE.md` §6: the visitor may be contacting the firm about an
extremely sensitive personal, family, legal, or professional matter.
Every CTA must read as calm and safe to click, never as pressure.

## Approved CTA language (use these, or close variants)

- **Primary:** Cuéntanos tu caso
- **Secondary:** Consulta confidencial · Hablar con un detective ·
  Solicitar valoración · Solicitar presupuesto · Contactar

## Do

- Pair the CTA with a one-line reassurance where space allows
  ("Respuesta discreta y confidencial", "Sin compromiso").
- Keep button label to 2-4 words, verb-first or noun-phrase, no
  exclamation mark.
- Make the next step obvious: what happens after they click (a form? a
  call?).

## Don't

- Don't use urgency language ("¡Actúa ahora!", "Plazas limitadas",
  "Última oportunidad") — this isn't a limited-time offer, it's a
  professional consultation.
- Don't stack multiple competing CTAs in the same viewport — one
  primary action per section.
- Don't promise a specific response time you can't confirm
  ("respuesta en 5 minutos") unless the user has verified it's real.

## Example rewrite

> Aggressive: "¡No esperes más! Contáctanos ahora y descubre la verdad
> hoy mismo."

> Correct: "Cuéntanos tu caso. Te responderemos de forma confidencial
> para valorar cómo podemos ayudarte."
