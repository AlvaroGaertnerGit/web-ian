# FAQ entry

Each entry answers a real question a potential client has before
contacting the firm (`CLAUDE.md` §11 "FAQ") — not a question invented to
showcase a keyword.

## Shape

1. **Question** — phrased the way a worried client would actually ask it
   ("¿Es legal que me investiguen?" not "Marco legal de la investigación
   privada").
2. **Answer** — 2-4 sentences, direct, no hedging paragraph before the
   actual answer.
3. If the honest answer is "depends on the case" — say that plainly and
   point to the consultation CTA, don't paper over it with a vague
   reassurance.

## Do

- Address the anxieties `CLAUDE.md` §6/§8 anticipates: legality, cost
  transparency, confidentiality, what evidence is actually obtainable.
- Keep legal-adjacent answers accurate and conservative — if an answer
  edges into a legal claim (what's admissible, what's guaranteed), run
  it through `legal-guardrails` before publishing.

## Don't

- Don't answer "¿Podéis acceder a su móvil/WhatsApp?" with anything
  other than a clear no + what the firm does instead — see
  `legal-guardrails` reference/claim-patterns.md for the exact framing.
- Don't invent a price range as a FAQ answer — use
  `[RANGO DE PRECIOS]` / direct to "Solicitar presupuesto" instead.

## Example rewrite

> Question: "¿Es legal contratar a un detective privado?"
> Answer: "Sí. La investigación privada en España es una actividad
> regulada. Trabajamos dentro del marco legal vigente y con interés
> legítimo acreditado en cada caso."

> Question: "¿Podéis acceder al teléfono o WhatsApp de otra persona?"
> Answer: "No. No accedemos a dispositivos ni cuentas de terceros —
> eso no es legal ni parte de nuestro trabajo. Obtenemos evidencia
> mediante seguimiento y documentación directa, dentro del marco legal."
